# Organization of the Sacratan Military

> **Purpose:** Canonical home for the organizational facts currently established about Sacratus's conventional human armed forces. Historical development belongs primarily in [`history.md`](history.md).

## An umbrella military institution

The **Sacratan Military** is the conventional human military layer of the system, not a synonym for the entire human population or civil administration.

It develops from several inherited Imperial military bodies rather than beginning as a single purpose-built army. The exact degree to which those bodies later become legally unified remains **OPEN**.

The Military jurisdiction is distinct from the Civil jurisdiction within Sacratan governance.

## Limitanei

The **Limitanei** originate as forces whose removal would leave the place or function they exist to defend dangerously exposed.

Their defining responsibilities include:

- fixed defenses;
- fortress and fortified-zone defense;
- enduring local garrison responsibilities;
- positions whose abandonment would immediately compromise the defended locality or strategic function.

The category begins as a practical assignment during Justinian's Reformation and later hardens into an institutional identity.

**OPEN:** The internal hierarchy, unit nomenclature, recruitment system, and degree of permanent geographic attachment within the Limitanei remain undeveloped.

## Comitatenses

The **Comitatenses** originate as forces that can be kept mobile and committed where the system most needs them.

They form the conventional field and strategic reserve: forces that can be shifted between threatened fronts rather than being structurally bound to one defended location.

Like the Limitanei, the category begins as a duty classification and becomes a durable institutional identity over time.

**OPEN:** The exact command structure, unit scales, basing, transport arrangements, and internal subdivisions of the Comitatenses remain undeveloped.

## Relationship between the two

The Limitanei/Comitatenses distinction is functional rather than a simple division between inferior and elite troops.

The central question is whether a formation can be moved without abandoning the thing it exists to defend. A locally indispensable formation belongs naturally to Limitanei duty; a force that can remain mobile belongs naturally to Comitatenses duty.

**OPEN:** Whether formations can transfer regularly between the two identities, whether all branches fit cleanly into the distinction, and how void or fleet forces are classified remain unresolved.

## Other inherited forces

Pre-Breaking Sacratus contains planetary defense forces, Guard-derived formations, naval forces, fortress personnel, and other conventional Imperial military bodies.

The later military grows from those inheritances. Canon does not yet define exactly how every pre-Breaking institution maps onto the mature Limitanei/Comitatenses system.

## Relationship to the Palatinae

The Palatinae do not constitute the mass army of Sacratus.

Even when only a few hundred battle-brothers remain, they continue to function as a strategic Astartes layer: command, boarding, counterattack, reinforcement of critical nodes, anti-Astartes and anti-daemonic action, and other missions where a small number of Space Marines can have disproportionate effect.

The conventional military provides the manpower for sustained system-scale defense.

## Other armed institutions

The Sacratan Military should not be treated as owning or absorbing every armed institution in the system.

The following remain distinct bodies with their own identities and jurisdictions:

- the **Palatinae**;
- **House Falkenrath**;
- the **Adepta Sororitas**;
- the **Mechanicus and Skitarii**.

Their forces may cooperate with the conventional military, particularly under emergency defense arrangements, without thereby becoming branches of it.

## Command and governance

The exact senior command hierarchy of the Sacratan Military remains **OPEN**.

Canon has not yet established:

- the title of the senior conventional military commander;
- how planetary and system-level commands relate;
- the office represented by the Military jurisdiction in the Consistorium;
- ordinary command authority versus Constantinian emergency authority under **Ultimum**;
- the precise relationship between ground, fleet, fortress, and other conventional commands.

These questions should be resolved when the faction is developed further rather than inferred from the Roman terminology alone.
