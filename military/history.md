# History of the Sacratan Military

> **Purpose:** Canonical home for the historical development of Sacratus's conventional human armed forces. For enduring roles and boundaries, see [`overview.md`](overview.md). For the currently established organizational model, see [`organization.md`](organization.md).

## Before the Breaking

Pre-Breaking Sacratus does not possess the later Long-Vigil military system in finished form.

Its conventional defense rests on ordinary Imperial institutions: planetary defense forces, Guard-derived formations, naval forces, fortress and station personnel, and other military bodies tied to the system.

They are not yet organized primarily around the later **Limitanei/Comitatenses** distinction.

## The Breaking

The Breaking in **100.M40** traps Sacratus without reliable access to the wider Imperial military system that had previously supplied reinforcement, replacement, authority, and strategic depth.

The immediate task is survival. Conventional forces fight alongside the Palatinae and the other armed institutions of the system while existing command arrangements are strained by isolation, catastrophic losses, and the sudden need to defend Sacratus without assuming outside relief.

The detailed order of battle during the Breaking remains **OPEN**.

## Justinian and the Reformation

The **Reformation** changes how Sacratus thinks about conventional military forces.

The emerging distinction is practical:

> **Can this formation be moved without abandoning the thing it exists to defend?**

Forces whose removal would expose the place or function they exist to protect increasingly become associated with **Limitanei** duty. Fixed defenses, fortress zones, and enduring local responsibilities fall naturally into this category.

Forces that can remain mobile and be committed wherever the system most needs them increasingly become **Comitatenses**. They provide a field force and strategic reserve that can be shifted between fronts.

The categories begin as assignments and duties rather than as an instantaneous replacement of every inherited Imperial formation. Over time, repeated use turns them into durable institutional identities.

The wider Reformation also rebuilds strategic depth within the system. Defense, logistics, repair, medical capacity, recruitment, and replacement must now function without dependable external replenishment. The rebuilding of **Sirmium** forms part of this new defensive architecture.

## High Vigil

During the High Vigil, Sacratus regains enough stability for conventional military institutions to operate with real strategic depth again rather than simply responding to immediate catastrophe.

The detailed expansion, force structure, campaigns, and relative strength of the Limitanei and Comitatenses during this period remain **OPEN**.

Conventional forces participate in the system's major wars, including **Dara**, where mortal Sacratan formations suffer heavy losses alongside the Palatinae in the successful destruction of the Chaos staging nexus.

## Contraction

After Ravenna and the end of the High Vigil, Sacratus can no longer sustain its previous military footprint.

Under **Leontios**, the Contraction includes deliberate reductions in:

- fleets;
- fortifications;
- **Comitatenses** deployments;
- **Limitanei** positions;
- long-term defense plans and the geography those plans assume can be held.

Many positions are abandoned not because the enemy has already conquered them, but because sustaining them would consume resources needed elsewhere.

The conventional military therefore contracts together with the rest of Sacratus rather than remaining an untouched institution guarding a shrinking state.

## Long Tension and Long Siege

During the later Low Vigil, unresolved political tensions remain, but the conventional military continues to carry the bulk of ordinary system defense.

By the **Long Siege**, most surviving strategic nodes are load-bearing. Defense becomes a continuous cycle of holding positions, escorting movement, relieving threatened locations, replacing losses, and keeping the system's remaining military geography connected.

The Palatinae remain strategically important, but their numbers fall into the low hundreds. Conventional human institutions therefore carry most system-scale defense through the siege.

The exact campaigns, commanders, formations, and casualty history of the Sacratan Military during this period remain **OPEN**.

## Silence and Reintegration

The Silence reduces organized external strategic Chaos pressure and allows cautious recovery, but it does not erase the institutions built during almost nineteen centuries of isolation.

When the Warp storm finally collapses in **999.M41**, wider Imperial military authorities return to a Sacratus whose conventional forces have developed under conditions that no longer exist.

**OPEN:** The post-Reintegration status of the Limitanei, Comitatenses, surviving local commands, and their relationship to outside Imperial military authority remains undeveloped.
