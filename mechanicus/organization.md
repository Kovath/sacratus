# Organization of the Sacratan Mechanicus

> **Purpose:** Canonical home for the institutional role and known organization of the Mechanicus of Sacratus. Detailed industrial systems belong primarily in [`industry.md`](industry.md); historical development belongs in [`history.md`](history.md); individual figures belong in [`figures/`](figures/README.md).

## Forge World authority

Sacratus is a **Forge World** governed by its own Mechanicus priesthood.

A **Fabricator-General** exists as the senior local Mechanicus authority. The detailed succession, internal political structure, subordinate offices, forge temples, and relationship among individual industrial domains remain **OPEN**.

The Sacratan Mechanicus remains institutionally distinct from the Palatinae, civil government, Ecclesiarchy, and military authorities throughout the Long Vigil.

## Consistorium and Concordat

The Mechanicus holds one of the five principal seats in the **Consistorium of Sacratus**.

Its participation reflects a recurring practical reality: defense decisions can require industrial output, transport, power, repairs, technical personnel, or the sacrifice of infrastructure, while Mechanicus survival depends in turn on food, labor, military protection, and system-wide logistics.

The later **Concordat of Sacratus** regularizes those overlaps without abolishing Mechanicus sovereignty.

The relationship becomes increasingly contentious during the Long Vigil because the emergency provision of **Ultimum** can allow the Constantine to claim extraordinary authority over matters deemed necessary to defense.

The Mechanicus does not dispute that industrial capability can be strategically necessary. The recurring dispute is how far that fact allows another jurisdiction to direct sacred Mechanicus decisions.

## The Fabricator-General and Constantinian authority

The Grand Calculus establishes the most important known example of this tension.

Leontios does not simply receive a voluntary offer from an entirely willing Fabricator-General to dismantle the Forge World's industrial base. He uses defense allocation, the Concordat, and Ultimum to force an accounting whose consequences the Mechanicus cannot practically ignore.

The formal right of Mechanicus authorities to make sacred industrial decisions therefore coexists with overwhelming system-level pressure.

A later Mechanicus objection captures the problem:

> **Coercion does not cease to be coercion merely because you leave us the formal right to refuse.**

The exact constitutional mechanics by which the Fabricator-General and other Mechanicus authorities participate in or contest Ultimum remain **OPEN** beyond what is established in the Grand Calculus.

## Manufactoria and industrial domains

The Mechanicus industrial presence is larger than any single forge complex.

Sacratus's importance historically rests on linked systems of production, repair, storage, transport, power, specialist labor, and technical knowledge. Its pre-Breaking specialization includes enormous intermediary production for wider Imperial campaigns and supply systems rather than only complete finished war machines.

Some associated infrastructure exists beyond the Forge World itself elsewhere in the Sacratus system, although ownership and jurisdiction should not be assumed automatically for every arsenal, yard, station, or industrial site.

The Long Vigil progressively reduces the amount of this network that can be maintained.

**OPEN:** Names and functions of individual forge complexes, major industrial orders, Titan or Legio presence if any, Skitarii organization, fleet assets, and ownership of specific system facilities remain undeveloped unless established elsewhere.

## Logis and accounting

The High Vigil and Grand Calculus establish the importance of **Logis** expertise within Sacratan Mechanicus history.

Industrial survival requires more than measuring theoretical output. The priesthood must account for maintenance burden, replacement cycles, supply dependencies, specialist personnel, repairability, transport, and the consequences of losing one process for every other process that depends upon it.

The career of [**Teren-44**](figures/Teren-44.md) demonstrates why this matters institutionally. As a comparatively mid-ranking Magos Logis during the mature High Vigil, he recognizes that accurate local reporting can still conceal dangerous system-wide dependencies. He later rises to Archmagos Logis sometime after Ravenna and is a senior internal Mechanicus figure during the Grand Calculus.

This is one reason nominal capacity becomes an increasingly dangerous measure during the Low Vigil. A machine or manufactorium that exists but cannot be supplied or repaired can consume more strategic capacity than it produces.

The detailed organization of Logis personnel and whether Sacratus develops distinctive Long-Vigil offices around this work remain **OPEN**.

## Knowledge custody

Technical knowledge itself becomes a strategic asset under isolation.

The historical **Context Loss**, formally **Operational Knowledge Continuity Failure**, demonstrates that the loss of a data archive, specialist, calibration lineage, ritual sequence, or practical understanding can disable equipment whose physical components remain intact.

Sacratan Mechanicus institutions therefore place increasing weight on preserving and reproducing context rather than merely preserving objects.

**OPEN:** The exact archive systems, teaching structures, redundancy practices, memory orders, and doctrinal authorities used to preserve technical knowledge remain to be developed.

## Post-Leontian compartmentalization

After the Grand Calculus, the Mechanicus becomes more cautious about how much capability it exposes to other Sacratan institutions.

This is not a declaration of independence or refusal to support Sacratus. It is an institutional defense against a lesson learned during Leontios's reign: information disclosed for coordination can become the basis for compulsory reallocation or sacrifice.

Under Andronikos, Mechanicus requests and offers are therefore more likely to be bounded by formal authority, narrow definitions, and compartmentalized information.

This behavior contributes materially to the **Long Tension**.

## Reintegration and Mars

The return of contact with Mars in **999.M41** reopens a hierarchy that had been practically unreachable for almost nineteen centuries.

The Sacratan Mechanicus regards its own authority and lineage as continuous. Wider Mechanicus institutions nevertheless have reason to verify records, recognize or contest offices, examine technical practices, and determine how local arrangements fit within restored Martian relationships.

The precise Reintegration settlement — including the status of the Fabricator-General, doctrinal review, external appointments, resource claims, and any disputes over technical legitimacy — remains **OPEN**.
