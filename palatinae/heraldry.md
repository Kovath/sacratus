# Palatinae Heraldry

> **Purpose:** Canonical home for the Chapter's visual identity, heraldic systems, markings, and the historical meaning attached to them. The [`overview.md`](overview.md) should summarize only the most important elements and defer here for detail.

## Chapter colors

The Palatinae use a dark Tyrian, aubergine, or wine-like **purple** as their primary color, with substantial **ivory/white** and **antique gold**. The intended appearance is rich and old rather than bright magenta or pink; black should not overwhelm the scheme.

## The Chi-Sword

The Chapter emblem is the **Chi-Sword**: a Chi (Χ) whose central vertical element is represented by a sword.

The emblem develops historically rather than being replaced wholesale. Old armor and relics may retain older forms, and multiple legitimate variants therefore coexist.

### Unbroken form

The oldest form is the plain Chi-Sword, without halo or rays. It is associated especially with Marines and wargear predating the Breaking. John Constantine's ancient Dreadnought retains this unhaloed form.

### Vigil-Born form

During the Long Vigil, a small disc or halo is added to the Chi-Sword, representing the Emperor's Light that remained visible through the storm.

### Radiant form

Later Long-Vigil heraldry develops a fully radiant or rayed form. Its richer sacred visual language becomes especially characteristic of the Chapter's later culture even as Sacratus becomes materially poorer.

## Armor provenance and repair

Palatinae culture places unusual weight on provenance, recovery, and continued service. Old heraldry is not automatically modernized merely because conventions change.

**OPEN:** The exact visual language for repaired armor, inherited plate, reclaimed components, campaign marks, and long service remains to be developed.

## Terminator heraldry

Every First Company battle-brother is entitled to the Crux Terminatus and has an assigned Terminator suit, though assignment does not guarantee immediate serviceability or deployment.

**OPEN:** Detailed Terminator, First Company, and individual suit heraldry remains to be developed.

## Dreadnoughts

Ancient Dreadnoughts provide an opportunity for older Palatinae heraldic states to survive visibly into later eras. John Constantine is the strongest established example through his retention of the pre-Breaking Chi-Sword.
