# Palatinae Timeline

> **Canon note:** This timeline focuses on the development of the Palatinae rather than the full political and military history of Sacratus. For the system-wide chronology, see [`../timeline.md`](../timeline.md). Unmarked entries are established canon; unresolved material is marked **PROVISIONAL**, **OPEN**, or **UNKNOWN IN-UNIVERSE**.

## Era Summary

| Period | Palatinae character |
|---|---|
| **Heresy / Second Founding** | XIII Legion defenders become the Palatinae and inherit the public mandate **DEFEND SACRATUS**. |
| **Pre-Breaking Chapter** | A broadly Codex Ultramarines successor develops close administrative ties to the growing Sacratan system and Augusta. |
| **The Breaking** | John Constantine invokes Ultimum; the Chapter suffers catastrophic losses; Constantine's Stand creates the defining memory of the Long Vigil. |
| **The Reformation** | Justinian rebuilds the Chapter for permanent isolation; the Varangian Guard and later Constantinian institutions take shape. |
| **The High Vigil** | The Chapter recovers, fights increasingly beyond immediate defensive lines, and begins to believe Sacratus can do more than survive. |
| **Ravenna / Stabilization** | Cassian dies with Ravenna; Basilios inherits a Chapter still carrying High-Vigil assumptions while losses begin to outpace recovery. |
| **The Contraction** | Leontios applies scarcity logic to the Chapter itself, suspending companies and reshaping reserve training. |
| **Long Tension / Long Siege** | The Palatinae remain in the low hundreds while sustaining centuries of boarding, counterattack, and critical-node warfare. |
| **Quiet Vigil** | Reduced external pressure finally gives recruitment and repair time to work in the Chapter's favor. |
| **Reintegration** | Primaris reinforcements and restored resources allow suspended formations to return while old Vigil adaptations are reevaluated. |

## Great Crusade and Horus Heresy

During the Horus Heresy, a XIII Legion detachment is stationed at Sacratus and participates in the system's defense.

The detachment's exact Great Crusade history, original Legion formation, and size at different points before the Second Founding remain **OPEN** unless later established elsewhere.

## Second Founding

The XIII Legion detachment becomes the **Palatinae**, a Second Founding Ultramarines successor.

The new Chapter receives the public mandate:

> **DEFEND SACRATUS.**

Separately, the first Chapter Master receives the secret inheritance concerning **Repository VIII**. The Repository is not the public explanation for why the Chapter is assigned to Sacratus.

The early Palatinae are broadly conventional sons of Guilliman. Their later distinctive institutions do not appear fully formed at founding.

## Pre-Breaking Development

Over the centuries before the Breaking, the Chapter's monastery-fortress becomes one of the centers around which Augusta develops. Administrative and logistical infrastructure grows around the Palatinae while pilgrimage grows around the nearby shrine commemorating the Emperor's visit.

The Chapter becomes increasingly involved in interinstitutional coordination because defense planning crosses Mechanicus, civil, military, and Ecclesiastical boundaries.

The first **Consistorium** precedes the formal Concordat of Sacratus. Later, the Concordat regularizes the system's overlapping jurisdictions and provides the constitutional environment in which **Ultimum** exists.

The Chapter remains broadly Codex in structure and doctrine during this era.

The earliest form of the Chapter badge is the plain, unhaloed **Chi-Sword**.

### John Constantine's succession chain

Long before the Breaking, Chapter Master John Constantine maintains an explicit chain of likely successors because a Chapter Master can die at any time. He does not intend to retire and does not treat succession as a ceremonial apprenticeship.

The principal sequence eventually runs:

**Varro → Aurelian → Marcellus → Valerian → Justinian**

Each predecessor dies before accession. Their detailed careers are recorded in the [Constantines biographies](constantines/README.md) where relevant to Justinian's formation.

## 100.M40 — The Breaking

A Warp storm encloses Sacratus and mass Chaos assault begins.

John Constantine invokes **Ultimum** immediately and assumes Autokrator authority for the defense of the system.

The Palatinae suffer catastrophic casualties across multiple fronts. Justinian, then John's chosen successor, is ordered to remain at Sirmium Prime rather than attempt to relieve John personally.

John and the defenders around him make **Constantine's Stand** on the approach to Repository VIII. The defending veterans die. John is catastrophically wounded and publicly believed dead.

A senior Librarian named **Theophanes** reaches John in his final moments. John entrusts him with the Repository key and the responsibility of carrying its charge to Justinian. Theophanes then arranges John's secret interment in a Dreadnought, giving the former Chapter Master one final role as Repository VIII's last guardian.

Theophanes does not reveal John's survival to Justinian. The Chapter does not know the truth as an institution, and Justinian dies centuries later still believing John was killed during the Breaking.

The Marines who survive from before the catastrophe later become known as the **Unbroken**.

## 100–~250.M40 — The Reformation

Justinian succeeds John and rebuilds the Palatinae for a world in which outside relief can no longer be assumed.

The Chapter increasingly functions as the strategic coordinator of Sacratan defense rather than attempting to replace mortal armies.

Important developments include:

- close integration with the emerging Comitatenses and Limitanei;
- rebuilding and reorganization around the Sirmium defenses;
- institutional adaptation to long-term scarcity;
- increasing emphasis on logistics, accounting, salvage, and recoverability;
- the emergence of the **Vigil-Born** as the first generations of Marines who never knew the pre-Breaking Imperium directly.

### Varangian Guard

The **Varangian Guard** is established during the early Reformation — exact date **OPEN**.

Exactly eight Marines are permanently assigned to the secret defense of Repository VIII. The Guard's sword-and-shield identity consciously recalls the veterans who died with John during Constantine's Stand.

The new office solves a practical problem: Repository security can no longer depend on temporary company assignments or the survival of particular officers.

### The Constantinian title

During the early Long Vigil, Sacratan mortals increasingly use **Constantine** as a title derived from John Constantine's memory.

The Chapter does not instantaneously rename Justinian at accession. The title becomes formal gradually as popular usage, Chapter memory, and institutional continuity merge.

By later centuries, Chapter Masters are formally counted as Constantines.

### Heraldic change

A halo or disc representing the Emperor's Light begins appearing with the Chi-Sword among Vigil-Born traditions.

Older armor is not repainted merely for consistency; pre-Breaking equipment can retain the simpler badge.

## ~250–417.M40 — Early High Vigil

Justinian's reforms begin to succeed.

The Chapter is no longer fighting only to prevent immediate extinction. Recruitment stabilizes, the system's military institutions become more competent, and Palatinae deployments can increasingly support deliberate offensive operations.

The reclamation of Ravenna during Justinian's reign is strategically modest but psychologically important. The Chapter participates in a system that can once again recover territory rather than only preserve what remains.

The Palatinae remain recognizably Codex-derived. The later severe company suspensions of the Low Vigil have not yet occurred.

## 417.M40 — Dara and the death of Justinian

The Palatinae and Sacratan forces attack the Chaos staging nexus at Dara.

Dara is a real offensive victory, but an exceptionally costly one. Significant Palatinae and Sacratan forces are lost in destroying the staging nexus and the corridor of pressure it supports. Justinian is among the dead.

The strategic gain is nevertheless enormous: Dara buys Sacratus substantial breathing room after centuries of pressure. That respite becomes one of the conditions that allows the next era to turn from consolidation toward outward expansion rather than immediately spending its strength on another defensive emergency. Dara proves that the rebuilt system can project force deliberately, but also that a victory on that scale cannot be purchased casually.

Cassian succeeds Justinian.

After Justinian's death, Theophanes reveals the truth of John Constantine's survival to Cassian. John is awakened and learns that Justinian ruled for 317 years and died without ever knowing that his predecessor remained alive.

This becomes the first full successor awakening of John after the secret established at the Breaking.

## 417–703.M40 — Mature High Vigil

Under Cassian, the Chapter's strategic posture becomes more proactive. The breathing room won at Dara gives Sacratus time and space in which that posture can matter.

The Palatinae participate in the expansion of the Chrysopolis defenses and the pushing of violence outward from the central system. Cassian's leadership style favors finding new options rather than choosing only between already-defined alternatives.

The **Great Ravenna** project represents the high point of the era. The Palatinae are part of a Sacratus confident enough to invest in long-term surplus rather than merely preserving reserves.

The project works for generations.

## 703.M40 — Exterminatus of Ravenna

Ravenna suffers catastrophic Warp collapse.

Theophanes dies during the crisis. Cassian concludes that the planet cannot be recovered without consuming Sacratus in the attempt.

He orders Exterminatus and remains on Ravenna.

Cassian's body, gene-seed, and personal relics are not recovered.

The loss is a deep rupture in Palatinae identity. A Chapter culture built around reclamation and recovery is forced to accept that some things must be destroyed rather than saved.

The High Vigil ends.

The later **Ash-Born** culture develops in the world that follows Ravenna. During the Low Vigil, the Chi-Sword also develops its later fully radiant form, adding richer sacred ornament as the material circumstances of Sacratus grow poorer.

## 703–761.M40 — Basilios and Stabilization

Basilios succeeds Cassian as Constantine IV.

The Chapter remains highly capable but increasingly unable to replace what it loses. Basilios's reign is defined less by transformation than by preventing Ravenna's consequences from cascading through every remaining defense.

At Chrysopolis in 761.M40, the defenders win a major engagement but discover that victory itself has become unaffordable.

Basilios dies.

The result is an important Palatinae lesson of the Low Vigil:

> A position can be held and still be unsustainable.

## 761.M40–096.M41 — The Leontian Contraction

Leontios becomes Constantine V and applies the logic of system-wide contraction to the Palatinae themselves.

The Chapter begins his reign at approximately **324 full battle-brothers** — **PROVISIONAL exact figure**.

The number should not be read as a simple survivor count from Ravenna. Recruitment and casualties continue throughout the preceding decades; the important point is that the Chapter can no longer sustain a full ten-company structure.

### Company suspensions

The **5th, 8th, and 9th Companies are suspended**.

Their standards and identities are preserved. Their Marines and capabilities are redistributed rather than the formations being declared dishonored or erased.

The surviving companies increasingly become:

- 1st — veteran and Terminator concentration;
- 2nd–4th — Battle Companies;
- 6th–7th — generalized reserve and training formations;
- 10th — neophyte and Scout pipeline with full-Astartes cadre.

The 6th and 7th cease to function primarily as narrow Codex reserve specialties. They become the place where full battle-brothers are cross-trained into complete Palatinae capable of filling whatever role the Chapter can still support.

### Combat-arm imbalance

Close-assault capability becomes especially difficult to sustain. Heavy weapons and ancient equipment can survive in the Armourium even while the Marines trained to use them disappear.

The Chapter's scarcity is therefore increasingly demographic rather than purely material.

### First Company and Terminators

The First Company retains the concentrated veteran tradition because it cannot be recreated merely by distributing old suits among younger Marines.

During the Low Vigil, veteran numbers fall far enough that surviving Terminator suits can outnumber the qualified brothers available to wear them. In this period every serving First Company battle-brother can therefore have a Terminator suit assigned, even though not every assigned suit is necessarily serviceable at a given moment and not every mission calls for Terminator plate.

This is a consequence of Low-Vigil demographic collapse, not a permanent entitlement or a rule that necessarily survives into the Chapter's current organization.

### Melitene

During Leontios's reign, the **Massacre of Melitene** becomes one of the most infamous Palatinae encounters with Sacratan civilians.

A local evacuation and enforcement crisis ends when Marines use chainswords against a charging crowd. Forty-three civilians are killed. No Palatinae die.

The event is tactically small and politically enormous. It becomes part of the Chapter's reputation as the institution that will choose the survival of Sacratus over any particular community when forced to do so.

## 096.M41 onward — Andronikos and the post-Leontian recoil

Andronikos succeeds Leontios.

The Chapter inherits not only scarcity but institutional distrust created by centuries of emergency authority. Under Andronikos, the Palatinae use Ultimum more cautiously and spend increasing effort preserving cooperation rather than simply compelling it.

The Chapter remains small enough that it cannot afford to become the permanent coercive mechanism for every Sacratan dispute.

## Late Andronikos — the trapped Space Hulk

**PROVISIONAL timing.**

An immense Space Hulk becomes effectively trapped within the Sacratan storm.

The Hulk creates a new class of Palatinae warfare: repeated boarding, reconnaissance, denial, salvage, quarantine, and withdrawal through an environment that is not consistently navigable.

The Chapter does not conquer the Hulk. Chaos eventually establishes a stable foothold within part of it, but Chaos does not control the whole structure.

Palatinae Terminator veterans become especially valuable in Hulk operations. The campaigns consume veterans and damage ancient suits while also creating opportunities for salvage.

At least one major Palatinae effort against the Hulk is intended to become a genuine Chapter failure rather than a hidden success — **OPEN** in its final details.

## 386–711.M41 — Manuel and the Long Siege

The exact transition from Andronikos to Manuel remains **OPEN**; the date **386.M41** is **PROVISIONAL**.

Under Manuel, organized Chaos pressure becomes effectively continuous.

The Palatinae spend centuries performing the kind of actions for which a small Astartes Chapter remains disproportionately useful: boarding, counterattack, relief, command, convoy defense, and emergency reinforcement of load-bearing positions.

The Chapter does not simply shrink every year. Recruitment continues, new Marines are elevated, and casualties continue. Gross turnover can be very high while net strength remains trapped in the low hundreds.

The **4th Company is likely suspended** during this era — **PROVISIONAL**.

The 6th and 7th become even more important as generalized training and operational-reserve formations.

Manuel's accomplishment is not restoring the Chapter to strength. He leaves his successor a Chapter that still functions as a Chapter.

## 711.M41 — Alexios succeeds

Alexios becomes Constantine VIII at roughly 273 years old.

The preferred working strength at accession is approximately **217 full battle-brothers** — **PROVISIONAL exact figure**.

The Long Siege continues after the succession without a meaningful pause.

## Quiet Vigil

**PROVISIONAL timing:** organized strategic Chaos attacks cease sometime around the later ninth century of M41, while the Warp storm and local corruption remain.

The cause is **UNKNOWN IN-UNIVERSE**.

For the Palatinae, the greatest resource created by the Silence is time.

Neophytes can mature without being consumed immediately by emergency deployments. Damaged equipment can remain in workshops long enough to be repaired properly. Veterans can train younger brothers rather than constantly filling gaps at the front.

The Chapter begins to recover gradually, though it does not return to pre-Breaking prosperity before Reintegration.

## 999.M41 — The Final Hell and end of the Long Vigil

During the final destabilization of the storm, daemonic manifestations erupt across Sacratus.

The Palatinae fight alongside the rest of the system through a short, catastrophic crisis rather than a conventional single-front campaign.

The Space Hulk is violently sheared as the Warp conditions sustaining its impossible structure collapse or change. The exact metaphysical cause remains **UNKNOWN IN-UNIVERSE**.

When the storm dissipates, the stars become visible again.

The Long Vigil ends after 1,899 years.

## Reintegration

The Palatinae encounter a transformed Imperium and accept Primaris reinforcements.

The Chapter's old emergency organization is no longer automatically appropriate merely because it survived for centuries.

As manpower recovers:

- the **4th** and **5th Companies** can return as Battle Companies;
- the **8th** and **9th Companies** can eventually return as specialized reserve formations;
- the **6th** and **7th** likely retain much of their generalized Palatinae training identity rather than reverting completely to pre-Vigil practice.

The Chapter's post-Vigil challenge is adaptation in the opposite direction: learning which habits of scarcity remain virtues and which have become liabilities once resources, external authority, and reinforcement exist again.
