# History of the Palatinae

> **Purpose:** This document is the narrative and causal history of the Palatinae: how the Chapter changed over time and why. For exact chronological reference, see [`timeline.md`](timeline.md). For the Chapter's enduring identity and present-day summary, see [`overview.md`](overview.md).

## Scope

This file is intentionally a skeleton for future expansion. It should eventually tell the Palatinae's history as a continuous account rather than as a list of dates or a collection of individual biographies.

The history should emphasize institutional and cultural transformation: how a conventional Second Founding Ultramarines successor became the Chapter that emerged from the Long Vigil.

## Second Founding and early Sacratus

_To be developed._

Topics include the XIII Legion inheritance, the founding mandate **DEFEND SACRATUS**, the Chapter's early relationship with Sacratus, and the gradual accumulation of logistical and institutional responsibilities before the Breaking.

## The Breaking

_To be developed._

The Breaking transforms the meaning of the Chapter's mandate. John Constantine's use of Ultimum, Constantine's Stand, and the loss of Imperial contact create the conditions from which later Palatinae institutions develop.

## The Reformation

_To be developed._

Under Justinian, the Chapter adapts from defending an Imperial system embedded in a larger Imperium to defending one that must reproduce its own military and institutional capacity. This period should explain the development of durable cooperation with Sacratan institutions and the emergence of Long-Vigil adaptations such as the Varangian Guard.

## The High Vigil

_To be developed._

The Chapter recovers sufficiently to move beyond immediate survival. Dara and the Cassianic period demonstrate that the Palatinae can again undertake strategic initiatives rather than merely absorb attacks.

## Ravenna and the Low Vigil

_To be developed._

The destruction of Ravenna ends the assumptions of the High Vigil. Basilios stabilizes the immediate aftermath; Leontios then subjects the Chapter itself to the same logic of contraction applied across Sacratus. Company suspensions, declining manpower, and changing military organization belong here as historical transformations rather than static organizational facts.

## Concordat Crisis and the Long Siege

_To be developed._

Under Andronikos and Manuel, the Palatinae must operate amid institutional distrust and increasingly continuous military pressure. The Chapter becomes progressively smaller while retaining a strategic role disproportionate to its numbers.

## The Quiet Vigil

_To be developed._

Under Alexios, organized external Chaos pressure eventually ceases for reasons **UNKNOWN IN-UNIVERSE**. The Chapter does not interpret the Silence as victory and recovers cautiously rather than attempting a return to High-Vigil expansion.

## Reintegration

_To be developed._

The end of the Long Vigil confronts the Palatinae with abundance, outside Imperial authority, Primaris reinforcement, and an Imperium profoundly different from the one they remember institutionally. Reintegration begins another period of adaptation rather than restoring a lost pre-Breaking state.
