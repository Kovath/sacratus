# Repository VIII

> **Purpose:** Canonical home for the established facts and deliberate unknowns surrounding Repository VIII. This file is intended to preserve constraints as much as exposition: material marked **UNKNOWN IN-UNIVERSE** is not a gap to be casually solved.

## Founding inheritance

Repository VIII is a secret inheritance given to the first Palatinae Chapter Master at the Chapter's founding.

It is **separate from** the public mandate:

> **DEFEND SACRATUS.**

The Repository does not explain publicly or secretly why the Palatinae were assigned to Sacratus, and the founding instruction does not prescribe the later institutions that grow around its custody.

The Chapter Master receives a separately authenticated Imperial instruction containing:

- the existence and location of Repository VIII;
- an order to defend it;
- an order that no one is to enter;
- a physical key associated with the Repository;
- the final instruction:

> **SACRATUS AWAITS ONLY THE EMPEROR.**

The authority of the instruction is accepted as authentic. The identity of the sender is **UNKNOWN IN-UNIVERSE**.

The message is not treated as proof that the Emperor personally authored or transmitted it.

## The key and the door

The physical key has never been used.

What it opens, how it functions, and whether it is sufficient by itself to enter Repository VIII are **UNKNOWN IN-UNIVERSE**.

The Repository's contents, purpose, internal architecture, and reason for being placed on Sacratus are likewise **UNKNOWN IN-UNIVERSE**.

No later emergency, including the Breaking, Ravenna, the Contraction, the Long Siege, or the Final Hell, has been accepted as justification to test the key or open the Repository.

The founding charge is understood literally:

> **No one enters.**

## Location and secrecy

Repository VIII lies hidden beneath or within the larger complex of **Augusta**.

Its existence and significance are not general Chapter knowledge. Custody is deliberately compartmentalized, and individuals involved in its defense do not necessarily possess identical information about its origin, key, guardian, or deeper history.

The Palatinae's wider culture values records and accurate accounting, creating an enduring tension around a trust whose security depends partly on preventing its full history from becoming ordinarily discoverable.

## The Breaking and John Constantine

During the Breaking, John Constantine's final defensive position lies on the approach to Repository VIII.

Publicly, **Constantine's Stand** is remembered as part of the defense of Augusta. Secretly, John's choice of ground also ensures that the enemy does not reach the Repository.

John is recovered catastrophically wounded. Theophanes, then a senior Librarian, receives the Repository key and enough of John's final knowledge to preserve the charge.

John is secretly interred in a Dreadnought and assumes one final continuing duty:

> **the final guardian of Repository VIII.**

His existence is concealed from Justinian, who rules for 317 years believing John died during the Breaking.

John is not maintained as an ordinary deployable Chapter Dreadnought. He is awakened rarely, and his continuing purpose is custody rather than strategic consultation.

## Custody after the Breaking

The institutions surrounding Repository VIII develop **after** the founding instruction rather than being specified by it.

The mature custody structure centers on three offices or bodies:

- the **Constantine**, as Chapter Master and inheritor of the Chapter's highest responsibility;
- the **Chartophylax**, as custodian of the Chapter's deepest restricted knowledge;
- the **eight Varangians**, as the permanent dedicated guard of the Repository.

John remains the final guardian beneath that living structure.

These roles do not imply that every participant knows every part of the secret. Repository custody remains compartmentalized even among those responsible for preserving it.

The **Varangian Guard** develops during the early Reformation because security can no longer depend on whichever company officers happen to be available. The formal Chartophylax role develops later, with Niketas becoming the first formal holder after Ravenna; Theophanes is its predecessor in function.

For the institutional details of those offices, see [`organization.md`](organization.md).

## Successor awakenings

John is not awakened automatically whenever a new Constantine accedes.

After Justinian's death, Theophanes reveals John's survival to Cassian and John is fully awakened for the first time by a successor who knows his identity. Later awakenings remain rare and tied to custody rather than ordinary requests for advice.

Leontios's awakening of John illustrates the mature relationship. He does not seek permission to open the Repository or attempt to exploit it during scarcity. He confirms only that the guardian and instruction remain intact.

The enduring principle is that a crisis does not change the charge merely because opening the Repository might be useful.

## Deliberate unknowns

The following are **UNKNOWN IN-UNIVERSE** and should remain unresolved unless explicitly changed:

- who sent the founding instruction;
- why Repository VIII exists;
- why it is located on Sacratus;
- what the Repository contains;
- what the physical key actually does;
- what conditions, if any, would constitute the Emperor's arrival for purposes of the final instruction;
- whether anyone outside the known custodial tradition possesses independent knowledge of the Repository.

Repository VIII should not become a general explanation for unrelated Sacratan mysteries, nor should later institutions be retroactively treated as part of a detailed founding plan. Its significance lies partly in the fact that the Palatinae have preserved a command for millennia without knowing why it was given.
