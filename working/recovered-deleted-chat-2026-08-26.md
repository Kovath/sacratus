## Material directly preserved in generated artifacts

The generated emblem sheets contain textual lore that appears to reflect the working state of the deleted conversation. These points are preserved here as **artifact evidence**, not as an assertion that they remain current canon:

- The Chapter emblem began in **M31** as the personal heraldry of Astartes **John Constantine**, initially without later religious significance.
- During **M32–M35**, the device was adopted as a Chapter badge, with laurels associated with Chapter/Ultramarine lineage.
- During **M36–M40**, the emblem acquired a halo as the Chapter's religious tradition deepened; the halo was associated with the Emperor's Light / the Astronomican.
- By **M41–present**, the depicted modernized form emphasized the sword as a symbol of duty and sacrifice.
- One artifact explicitly labels **Constantine I** as a Dreadnought.
- One artifact describes the **Varangian Guard** as eight members who know of **Repository VIII**, do not speak of it, and do not leave.
- Sacratan religious/heraldic imagery associates the Chapter emblem with the **Astronomican**, endurance, and the Emperor's light through the Long Vigil.
- `Lux Perstat` / **“The Light Endures”** appears as motto material.
- Artifact naming also includes **“Chi-Sword of the Vigil”** and **“Chi-Sword of His Light.”**

These statements may overlap with, predate, or conflict with later accepted lore. The canonical repository should win whenever there is a discrepancy.

## What could not be recovered

A forensic search of the 2026-09-11 export found no raw message tree or full-text copy of conversation `6a8ec986-43b4-83e8-a4e7-3251fe871a56` in the exported conversations or `chat.html`.

The surviving artifact provenance proves the thread existed, but does not recover the missing discussion between its opening prompt and the later image-generation activity. Therefore the following cannot be established from the export alone:

- how long the original textual discussion was;
- the exact wording of lore proposals and decisions;
- which ideas were accepted, rejected, or revised inside that lost chat;
- whether every piece of text embedded in a generated image represented accepted lore versus exploratory design material.
