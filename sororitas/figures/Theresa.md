# Canoness Theresa

## Role

**Canoness Theresa** is the foundational named Adepta Sororitas figure of the early Long Vigil.

She becomes important after the **Breaking**, when isolation turns the continued existence of the Sororitas from an ordinary Imperial assumption into an institutional problem that must be solved locally.

## Reformation

During the Reformation, Theresa expands Sororitas institutions and establishes the generational continuity needed for the Sisters to persist without dependable outside reinforcement.

Her importance is therefore institutional as much as military. The Sororitas cannot survive the Long Vigil as a dwindling collection of pre-Breaking veterans; they must remain capable of producing trained successors, preserving discipline and doctrine, and maintaining both armed and service roles across generations.

Theresa's work helps turn the isolated Sororitas presence into a durable Sacratan institution.

## Relationship to wider Sacratus

Theresa belongs to the same Reformation-era movement that turns emergency cooperation among Sacratan institutions into durable practice, but she is not simply an Ecclesiastical subordinate of Justinian or a Sororitas equivalent of a Palatinae officer.

The Sisters retain their own identity and authority while participating in the shared defense and survival of Sacratus.
