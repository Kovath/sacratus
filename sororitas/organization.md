# Organization of the Sacratan Sororitas

> **Purpose:** Canonical home for currently established institutional boundaries and organizational facts concerning the Adepta Sororitas in Sacratus. Historical development belongs primarily in [`history.md`](history.md).

## Institutional boundary

The **Sacratan Sororitas** are not synonymous with the Sacratan Ecclesiarchy.

The Ecclesiarchy predates the Sororitas presence and encompasses the broader clergy, shrine administration, pilgrimage, and Ecclesiastical jurisdiction of the system. The Sororitas are a distinct institution within that religious world.

The exact constitutional relationship between the Sororitas and the **Ecclesiastical seat of the Consistorium** remains **OPEN**. The Sisters should not be assumed to own, automatically occupy, or exclusively represent that seat.

## Orders and branches

A Sororitas presence exists in Sacratus by the Breaking and survives throughout the Long Vigil.

Established material includes combat-capable Sisters and **Hospitallers**, but canon has not yet established the complete internal composition of the Sacratan Sororitas.

**OPEN:**

- the formal Order or Orders represented in Sacratus;
- whether the local presence belongs to one Order or several;
- names, heraldry, colors, convents, and external parent relationships;
- the detailed division between Orders Militant, Hospitallers, and any other Sororitas branches;
- force strength at any point in the Long Vigil.

## Canoness Theresa

**Canoness Theresa** is the earliest and most important named Sororitas leader currently established.

During the Reformation, she expands Sororitas institutions and establishes the generational continuity necessary for them to survive without dependable external reinforcement.

Her exact office, Order affiliation, subordinates, succession arrangements, and the institutions directly under her command remain **OPEN**.

## Generational continuity

The Long Vigil requires the Sororitas to remain institutionally reproducible rather than merely preserve a dwindling body of pre-Breaking Sisters.

Theresa's Reformation-era work establishes that continuity as a durable feature of the Sacratan Sororitas. The exact recruitment sources, novitiate system, training institutions, and relationship to wider Ecclesiastical education remain **OPEN**.

## Military and humanitarian roles

The Sororitas remain an armed Imperial institution and participate in the defense of Sacratus without becoming part of the conventional Sacratan Military.

Their established non-battlefield roles also include Hospitaller care, rites, child evacuation, camps, preservation of names and records, and presence among displaced or abandoned populations.

The exact command relationships during joint operations with the Palatinae, conventional military, or other armed institutions remain **OPEN**.

## Reintegration

Reintegration reconnects the Sacratan Sororitas with outside Orders and Ecclesiastical authorities after almost nineteen centuries of separate development.

**OPEN:** the resulting questions of Order affiliation, external command, local seniority, recognition of Sacratan offices, and any reorganization required after contact is restored.
