# Sacratan Sororitas — Overview

> **Canon note:** This file is the high-level canonical reference for the Adepta Sororitas presence in Sacratus. Unmarked statements are established canon. Material explicitly marked **OPEN** retains that status.

## Scope

The **Sacratan Sororitas** are the Adepta Sororitas institutions and Sisters serving within the Sacratus system.

They are not the whole Sacratan Ecclesiarchy. The Ecclesiarchy's presence in Sacratus predates the Sororitas and includes the clergy, shrine administration, pilgrimage traditions, and wider religious jurisdiction surrounding places such as **Augusta**. The Sororitas develop within that older religious landscape while retaining their own institutional identity.

The exact Order or Orders represented in Sacratus, their formal names, external parent relationships, and the date and circumstances of their original establishment remain **OPEN**.

A Sororitas presence exists by the Breaking. It includes combat-capable Sisters as well as non-militant service represented in established canon by **Hospitallers**. The exact composition and relative strength of those elements remain **OPEN**.

## Long-Vigil role

The Sisters become one of the institutions through which Sacratus preserves both armed faith and human continuity during almost nineteen centuries of isolation.

They fight as part of Sacratus's wider defense without becoming a branch of the conventional military or the Palatinae. Their importance also extends beyond battlefield strength. Sororitas institutions provide medical care, rites, evacuation support, camps, records of names, and continuing presence among populations under extreme stress.

This becomes especially important during the Low Vigil, when material survival and political legitimacy increasingly diverge.

## Theresa and the Reformation

**Canoness Theresa** is the foundational named Sororitas figure of the early Long Vigil.

During the Reformation, she expands Sororitas institutions and helps establish the generational continuity required for the Sisters to remain a functioning institution without dependable reinforcement from the wider Imperium.

The exact structure she creates, her Order affiliation, and the details of her career remain **OPEN**.

## Faith during isolation

A central Sacratan Sororitas formulation is:

> **He has not abandoned us.**

This is not a promise that rescue is imminent, nor proof that the faithful will be protected from catastrophe. Its meaning is obligation: the Emperor, Terra, and the Imperium still exist, therefore duty still exists.

The theology is compatible with the wider Sacratan experience of the Astronomican as the distant **Light**, but the Sororitas expression is specifically religious and pastoral rather than merely strategic.

## Ravenna, Contraction, and legitimacy

The catastrophe of **Ravenna** is a major Sororitas trauma and point of memory. The exact Sororitas deployments, casualties, and institutional losses during the crisis remain **OPEN**.

During **Leontios's Contraction**, the Sisters become increasingly associated with the people and communities Sacratus can no longer materially save. They provide Hospitallers, rites, child evacuation, camps, names, and presence even where the state has decided that continued defense or support is impossible.

The contrast enters popular memory as:

> **When Constantine counted us, we counted on the Sisters.**

This does not mean the Sororitas can solve the material problems confronting Leontios. It means that necessity and legitimacy are remembered differently.

The **Massacre of Melitene** sharpens that distinction. Formal records describe a civil disturbance; popular and Sororitas memory calls it a massacre.

## Political weight

By the **Concordat Crisis / Long Tension**, the Sororitas possess substantial moral and political legitimacy created in part by their conduct during the Contraction.

They push back against broad Constantinian claims and emergency precedents without ceasing to be loyal defenders of Sacratus. Their resistance is part of a wider post-Leontian recoil against institutions allowing temporary defense authority to become unlimited habit.

The exact constitutional relationship between the Sororitas, the broader Ecclesiastical jurisdiction, and the Ecclesiastical seat in the Consistorium remains **OPEN**. The Sororitas should not be assumed to own or automatically occupy that seat.

## Reintegration

Reintegration reconnects the Sacratan Sororitas with an outside Ecclesiarchy and Adepta Sororitas that have continued to develop for almost nineteen centuries.

The exact treatment of the local institutions, Order relationships, authority, seniority, and customs after contact is restored remains **OPEN**.
