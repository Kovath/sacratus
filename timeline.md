# Constantinian Timeline of Sacratus

> **Status:** Tentative working chronology. Dates and labels marked as provisional or loose are expected to move as later eras are fleshed out.

## Pre-Constantinian Foundations

| Date | Constantine / Era | Major developments |
|---|---|---|
| **Great Crusade / Horus Heresy** | Pre-Constantinian | Sacratus is established as a major Mechanicum logistics and industrial nexus. The Emperor visits once; the circumstances and purpose are later disputed. Augusta eventually grows around the remembered site. A XIII Legion detachment defends Sacratus during the Heresy. |
| **Second Founding** | Early Palatinae | The XIII Legion detachment becomes the **Palatinae**, assigned the public mandate **DEFEND SACRATUS**. The first Chapter Master separately receives the secret inheritance concerning Repository VIII. |
| **~247.M39** *(provisional)* | — | Birth of **John Constantine**. |
| **836.M39** *(provisional)* | — | Birth of **Justinian**. |

## Constantine I — John / The Breaking

| Date | Event | Major developments |
|---|---|---|
| **100.M40** | **The Breaking** | A Warp storm encloses Sacratus. The stars vanish or distort while the Astronomican remains visible as the Light. Massive Chaos assaults begin. John invokes **Ultimum** and assumes Autokrator authority. Justinian holds Sirmium Prime. John makes **Constantine's Stand** before Repository VIII and is catastrophically wounded. Publicly he dies; secretly he is interred as a Dreadnought and becomes the Repository's final guardian. |

## Constantine II — Justinian

| Date | Event | Major developments |
|---|---|---|
| **100.M40** | Accession | Justinian succeeds John, believing him dead. The **Reformation** begins. |
| **~100–250.M40** | **Reformation** | Sacratus reorganizes for permanent isolation. Horizontal cooperation develops between Palatinae, Mechanicus, Ecclesiarchy/Sororitas, civil authorities and the military. The Comitatenses and Limitanei emerge. Sirmium is rebuilt. Theresa expands Sororitas institutions and generational continuity. The Varangian Guard gradually formalizes. |
| **126.M40** | — | Birth of **Cassian**, the first future Constantine born entirely after the Breaking. |
| **~250.M40 onward** | **High Vigil begins** | The reforms work. Population and industry recover. Sacratus stops merely surviving and begins living again. |
| **~270–300.M40** *(loose)* | First Ravenna reclamation | Ravenna is retaken but remains devastated and only modestly productive. Its symbolic value is enormous: **what was lost can be returned**. |
| **391.M40** *(provisional)* | — | Birth of **Basilios**. |
| **417.M40** | **Dara** | Sacratus identifies and attacks the Chaos staging nexus at Dara. The campaign is a genuine major victory that buys centuries of breathing room. Justinian dies during the operation. |

## Constantine III — Cassian

| Date | Event | Major developments |
|---|---|---|
| **417.M40** | Accession | Cassian succeeds at roughly 291 years old. Theophanes finally reveals that John survives. John awakens and learns that Justinian ruled for 317 years and died without ever knowing the truth. |
| **417–~600s.M40** | **Mature High Vigil** | Cassian's proactive reign. The Chrysopolis Belt and outer strategic infrastructure expand. Violence is pushed outward. Sacratus increasingly believes it can shape its own future. |
| **474.M40** | — | Birth of **Leontios**. |
| **Mid/late Cassian reign** | **Great Ravenna** | Cassian and Archmagos Teren-44 undertake a massive redevelopment of Ravenna. It succeeds for generations: food surplus grows, rationing eases, population expands and industrial capacity is freed. |
| **703.M40** | **Fall of Ravenna** | Accumulated Warp instability becomes a nonlinear catastrophe. Theophanes dies. Cassian concludes that Ravenna is irrecoverable and orders **Exterminatus**, remaining on the world. Billions die. The **High Vigil ends**. |

## Constantine IV — Basilios

| Date | Event | Major developments |
|---|---|---|
| **703.M40** | Accession | Basilios succeeds Cassian. Niketas becomes the formally institutionalized **Chartophylax** after Ravenna's emergency transmission of Repository custody. |
| **703–761.M40** | **Stabilization** | Basilios spends 58 years preventing the consequences of Ravenna from cascading through the system. High-Vigil assumptions still linger and many believe recovery remains possible. |
| **761.M40** | **Chrysopolis** | A major Chaos incursion is defeated, but the cost demonstrates that Chrysopolis can no longer be sustainably restored. Basilios dies. Sacratus finally recognizes that the High Vigil is truly over. |

## Constantine V — Leontios

| Date | Event | Major developments |
|---|---|---|
| **761.M40** | Accession | Leontios succeeds at roughly 287. The great **Contraction** begins, remembered more bitterly in popular memory as the **Butchering**. |
| **761–096.M41** | **The Contraction** | Leontios systematically reduces Sacratus to what can actually be sustained. Chrysopolis is abandoned. The **Grand Calculus** forces Mechanicus contraction. Antiochia is heavily pruned. Sirmium II is dismantled. The military footprint shrinks. Whole loyal communities are sacrificed because the system cannot sustain them. |
| **During V** | **Massacre of Melitene** | An evacuation and enforcement crisis ends with Palatinae killing **43 civilians** with chainswords. No Palatinae casualties are suffered. Numerically minor, symbolically enormous. Sororitas reputation as those who remain with the abandoned grows sharply. |
| **During V** | **Leontian Reorganization** | The Palatinae themselves are cut. Current working strength at accession is **~324 full battle-brothers**. The **5th, 8th and 9th Companies are suspended**. The 6th and 7th evolve into generalized training and reserve formations. |
| **096.M41** | Death | Leontios dies in Augusta after a prolonged physical decline rather than in battle. Sacratus is smaller, poorer and politically traumatized, but the material hemorrhage has largely slowed. |

## Constantine VI — Andronikos

| Date | Event | Major developments |
|---|---|---|
| **096.M41** | Accession | Andronikos inherits the **post-Leontian recoil**. Institutions begin openly resisting the degree of cooperation and emergency intrusion normalized under Constantine V. |
| **096–mid/late M41** | **Concordat Crisis / Long Tension** | Mechanicus compartmentalizes. Civil authorities conceal reserves and demographic detail. Sororitas push back against Constantinian claims. Cooperation fails more often than loyalty does. Andronikos avoids Ultimum whenever possible and rebuilds enough trust for the system to function without actually resolving its constitutional disputes. |
| **Late Andronikos** *(date TBD)* | **Space Hulk arrival** | An immense anomalous Space Hulk becomes effectively trapped within the Sacratan storm. It is spatially and temporally unstable, with changing populations of Orks, unknown xenos and fauna, Warp phenomena, Chaos forces and other hazards. Chaos eventually establishes a stable foothold within part of it. |
| **Late Andronikos** | Hulk wars / strategic deterioration | The Hulk becomes a forward operating environment for Chaos. Chaos itself is visibly fighting things within it. Sacratus conducts costly boarding, salvage and denial campaigns. The Hulk is not the sole cause of the coming war, but it materially alters the strategic geography of the system. |
| **386.M41** *(provisional)* | Succession | Andronikos dies or yields the office amid worsening conditions. The exact transition remains to be developed. |

## Constantine VII — Manuel

| Date | Event | Major developments |
|---|---|---|
| **386.M41** *(provisional)* | Accession | Manuel succeeds at roughly 302. He is chosen less for a singular genius than because he is the best viable commander left. |
| **386–711.M41** | **The Long Siege** | Chaos pressure becomes effectively continuous. The Hulk is one major forward base and disruptor, but the war is system-wide. Sacratus has already contracted near minimum viable geography; most remaining nodes are load-bearing. Manuel's reign becomes centuries of holding, repairing, escorting, relieving and replacing losses. |
| **During VII** | Further Palatinae contraction | The **4th Company is likely suspended** as manpower falls further. Chapter strength fluctuates through continual recruitment and casualties rather than declining in a straight line. Hundreds of Marines may die and hundreds more be elevated over VI–VII while the Chapter remains in the low hundreds. Terminator armour increasingly outnumbers available veterans. |
| **438.M41** | — | Birth of **Alexios** during the Long Siege. Siege conditions are simply normal life to his generation. |
| **711.M41** | Succession | Manuel dies. The siege does not pause for the change of Constantine. |

## Constantine VIII — Alexios

| Date | Event | Major developments |
|---|---|---|
| **711.M41** | Accession | Alexios succeeds Manuel at roughly 273. Preferred working strength at accession is **~217 full battle-brothers**. The Long Siege simply continues. |
| **~711–830/850.M41** *(very loose)* | **Late Long Siege** | Alexios initially rules as another Siege Constantine. Palatinae manpower approaches its demographic floor while mortal institutions bear most system-scale defense. |
| **~830/850.M41 onward** *(loose)* | **The Silence / Quiet Vigil** | Organized strategic Chaos offensives abruptly cease. The Warp storm remains. Local corruption, cults, daemonic incidents and the Hulk's dangers do **not** vanish. Chaos forces aboard the Hulk appear increasingly occupied by internal warfare, withdrawal, fratricide or unknown pressures, but the evidence is contradictory. No causal explanation is established. |
| **Quiet Vigil** | Cautious recovery | Sacratus slowly recovers. Starving becomes hungry rather than prosperous. Weeks of strategic reserve become achievements. Palatinae manpower rises gradually because neophytes can mature without immediately being consumed by emergencies. Limited Hulk operations continue, but nobody conquers it. |
| **999.M41** | **Final Destabilization** | The surrounding Warp phenomenon catastrophically destabilizes over hours or days. Daemonic manifestations erupt throughout Sacratus. The Hulk undergoes violent spatial and translation shearing: sections disappear, translate, separate or remain as conventional wreckage. |
| **999.M41** | **End of the Long Vigil** | Mass daemonic manifestations collapse. The Warp storm dissipates. For the first time since 100.M40, **the stars are visible again**. Much of the Hulk is simply gone; scattered realspace wreckage remains. No satisfying causal explanation exists. |
| **999.M41 onward** | **Reintegration** | Sacratus encounters the wider Imperium again: the Great Rift-era Imperium, Guilliman's reforms, Primaris reinforcements, Mars, the Administratum, the Inquisition, renewed Ecclesiastical hierarchy and Imperial military authorities. The 1,899-year emergency finally has to confront ordinary Imperial law and jurisdiction again. |
| **Early Reintegration** | Restoration | Primaris and other reinforcements are accepted. Suspended companies begin to return as manpower allows. The 4th and 5th are restored; eventually the 8th and 9th can return as specialized reserve formations. The 6th and 7th likely retain much of their thousand-year Ash-Born generalized training identity. Ultimum may formally conclude. |

## Constantinian Sequence

1. **Constantine I — John**: The Breaking
2. **Constantine II — Justinian**: Reformation → early High Vigil
3. **Constantine III — Cassian**: mature High Vigil → Ravenna
4. **Constantine IV — Basilios**: Stabilization
5. **Constantine V — Leontios**: Contraction
6. **Constantine VI — Andronikos**: Long Tension → Hulk / strategic deterioration
7. **Constantine VII — Manuel**: Long Siege
8. **Constantine VIII — Alexios**: Long Siege → Silence → Reintegration

## Strongest Current Date Anchors

The dates currently treated as the strongest historical anchors are:

- **100.M40** — Breaking; John → Justinian
- **417.M40** — Dara; Justinian → Cassian
- **703.M40** — Ravenna; Cassian → Basilios
- **761.M40** — Chrysopolis; Basilios → Leontios
- **096.M41** — Leontios → Andronikos
- **386.M41** — Andronikos → Manuel *(provisional)*
- **711.M41** — Manuel → Alexios
- **999.M41** — End of the Long Vigil / Reintegration

The exact placement of the Space Hulk's arrival, the beginning of the Long Siege, the beginning of the Silence, and several birth dates remain deliberately flexible.
