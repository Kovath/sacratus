# History of House Falkenrath in Sacratus

> **Purpose:** Canonical home for the causal history of the Falkenrath branch trapped within Sacratus. For its enduring identity and current scale, see [`overview.md`](overview.md). For the surviving household structure and strength, see [`organization.md`](organization.md).

## Before the Breaking

House Falkenrath is a **Questor Mechanicus** Knight House based outside the Sacratus system.

Shortly before the Breaking, a Falkenrath detachment is present in Sacratus. It comprises roughly **twenty to twenty-five Knights** together with the sacristans, retainers, technical personnel, families, and household support required to sustain a Knight formation away from its principal seat.

This is not the relocation of the entire House and does not create a local Knight World.

**OPEN:** The exact date, mission, circumstances, and intended duration of the detachment's presence remain undeveloped.

## The Breaking

When the storm encloses Sacratus in **100.M40**, the detachment is unable to return to its parent House.

The Knights and the human household around them become a stranded branch inside a system that can no longer assume replacement pilots, new Knight suits, major external repair resources, or ordinary contact with the wider Falkenrath lineage.

The support population trapped with the Knights is therefore as important to continuity as the engines themselves. Sacristans, retainers, technical personnel, and families provide the institutional and generational depth from which the branch can continue to exist rather than becoming a one-generation military formation.

## The Long Vigil

Across the Long Vigil, Falkenrath remains a distinct Knight lineage rather than expanding into a full local Knight House with its own Knight World.

Its principal military role is as a **strategic reserve**. The Knights are too valuable and too difficult to replace to be treated as ordinary garrison assets across the entire system. They are instead preserved for situations in which Knight-scale force can produce disproportionate strategic value.

The branch nevertheless suffers cumulative attrition across nearly nineteen centuries of warfare and isolation.

The exact chronology of Knight losses is **OPEN**. No fixed linear decline is established, and the current lore does not assign particular losses to Dara, Ravenna, the Contraction, the Long Siege, or other individual campaigns unless later developed explicitly.

By the later Long Vigil and Reintegration era, approximately **five to six Knights remain operational**.

## Continuity without the parent House

Falkenrath's isolation creates an unusual form of continuity.

The branch remains part of a Questor Mechanicus lineage, yet every practical question of succession, maintenance, household continuity, and local obligation must be answered without routine access to the parent House for almost nineteen centuries.

Its response is generally **highly conservative**. Falkenrath holds closely to the traditions and identity it possessed before the Breaking, treating continuity with the pre-isolation House as something to be preserved rather than allowing the stranded branch simply to become culturally Sacratan.

Its Germanic and Gothic vocabulary remains one visible expression of that continuity. The exact evolution of individual rites, customs, hierarchy, and its relationship with the Sacratan Mechanicus remain **OPEN**.

## Reintegration

When the Long Vigil ends in **999.M41**, the Sacratan branch encounters its parent House again after almost nineteen centuries of separation.

The parent House **warmly receives** the returned branch. Reintegration is therefore not a story of rejection by a lineage that considers the Sacratan survivors illegitimate.

The difficulty is cultural distance. The stranded branch has spent almost nineteen centuries deliberately preserving an old Falkenrath identity while the parent House has continued developing beyond the storm. Both remain Falkenrath, but neither is exactly the House the other remembers.

**OPEN:** The detailed cultural accommodations, questions of authority and obligation, and final long-term settlement remain undeveloped.
